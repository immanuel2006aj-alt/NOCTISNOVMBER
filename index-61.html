02 / The visibility toolkit

SEO, AEO, GEO — what do they actually mean?

These terms overlap. They are not magic buttons or separate ranking systems. Here is what each discipline is trying to improve.

⌕

SEO · Search Engine Optimization

Make your pages technically accessible, relevant and useful so search engines can understand them and people can find them.

  • Technical SEO, crawling and indexing
  • Keyword research and search intent
  • On-page content and internal links
  • Authority, reputation and useful backlinks
⌖

Local SEO

Help people in a specific area discover and evaluate your real-world business.

  • Google Business Profile accuracy
  • Local landing pages and location relevance
  • Reviews, photos, opening hours and contact consistency
  • Local citations and map visibility
?

AEO · Answer Engine Optimization

Structure genuinely helpful information so answer-focused experiences can understand it and people can get clear answers.

  • Question-led pages and concise explanations
  • Useful FAQs based on real customer questions
  • Clear definitions, comparisons and how-to content
  • Evidence, authorship and source transparency
✧

GEO · Generative Engine Optimization

Improve the chance that accurate, distinctive business information can be discovered and referenced in AI-powered search experiences.

  • Original expertise and first-hand examples
  • Clear entity, service and brand information
  • Credible third-party mentions and citations
  • Accessible pages with verifiable facts
▣

E-commerce & Shopping SEO

Help product and category pages appear for high-intent shopping searches and make product choices easier.

  • Product titles, descriptions and category architecture
  • Product data and eligible structured data
  • Image optimisation and Merchant Center readiness
  • Faceted navigation and duplicate URL control
▶

Image, Video & Social Search

Make visual content understandable, accessible and useful across platforms where customers research brands.

  • Descriptive image names, alt text and context
  • Video titles, descriptions and transcripts
  • Useful short-form educational content
  • Consistent brand information across channels
✦
AEO / GEO truth from Google

Google's current guidance says its AI Search features use the same foundational SEO principles. There is no special AI schema, secret file or guaranteed shortcut needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. Focus on crawlable pages, original helpful content and clear evidence — not hype.
